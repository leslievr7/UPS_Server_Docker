What makes a good sentinel

Sentinels are how the server notices a power cut.

  • {{ ui.icon('plug') }}

    Plugged into the wall, not the UPS

    So it goes quiet when the power does.

  • {{ ui.icon('power') }}

    Always on

    Smart plugs, TV boxes and speakers work well. Phones and laptops come and go.

  • {{ ui.icon('radio-tower') }}

    Two or three, in different rooms

    One quiet device might just be unplugged. All of them at once means a power cut.
